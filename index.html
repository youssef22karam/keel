      A ship of your own.

      KEEL turns a budget and a seed into a complete procedural vessel. No downloaded ship models. No two briefs alike.

      01
      Start with a possibility

      Enter any budget from $100. Even a one-dollar change redraws the hull, arrangement, and character.

      02
      Find your favorite

      Randomize for a new vessel at the same budget. The same cost and seed always recreate your design.

      03
      Get closer

      Orbit the exterior, then step inside. Walk the wheelhouse, climb to furnished crew decks, click the radar to change range, open the roof, or strip the wireframe.

      04
      Fit her out

      Toggle the fishing rod rig and outriggers on or off, and watch propellers, radar, wipers, oars, crane hooks, and pennants move.
